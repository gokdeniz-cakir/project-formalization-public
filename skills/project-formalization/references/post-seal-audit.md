# Post-Result Audit

Begin after A and B have finished and their final states are stable. A useful snapshot or commit is enough in `agile` mode; paired critic acceptance and exhaustive coordinator reruns are `audit`-profile requirements.

## Compare Arms

Compare claims, properties, traces, affected objects, source events, bounds, and replay inputs. Preserve disagreements and uncertainty. Convergence on one mechanism is corroboration, not two vulnerabilities.

## Novelty

Only now may the coordinator search target advisories, CVE databases, issues, changelogs, later commits, and patches. Keep a short list of material sources and classify the result as known same mechanism, known adjacent issue, apparently novel after bounded search, or inconclusive.

## Retrospective Match

Compare the actual trace against the fixing patch and advisory. Identify the allocation or initialization, invalidation/free or bounds transition, later use/access, affected production object, and caller sequence. A different real defect must not be labeled as the retrospective CVE.

## Severity

Assess reachability, attacker influence, memory primitive, demonstrated read/write/disclosure/crash/control-flow effect, reliability, configuration, privileges, mitigations, and affected versions. A UAF is not proof of arbitrary code execution.

## Confidentiality

Produce local research notes and draft report material. Do not contact maintainers, submit, email, publish, merge confidential branches, or push results without a separate explicit user request.
